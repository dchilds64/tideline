Asset credits

Tideline is a fictional setting. These creators are credited for their work; no endorsement is implied.

Operator character

  • Military tactical suit (LowPolyGameReady) by DanlyVostok: mesh, skeleton and textures, CC BY 4.0.
  • FREE [Military Soldier] RIGGED by BAMEN: source animation rig, CC BY 4.0. Its body mesh is not included in the game.
  • Universal Animation Library by Quaternius: animation clips, CC0 1.0.
  • cktang88 / 3d-fps: source GLB conversion and prior animation retargeting. Tideline retargets selected clips to the tactical suit, removes its displaced headset, bakes ground correction and adds runtime grip and aim adjustments. Texture colors vary by faction.

Coastal materials

Poly Haven provides Coast Sand 02, Concrete Wall 003, Rock Boulder Cracked, Weathered Planks and Denim Fabric under CC0 1.0.

First-person hands and rifle

These assets use CC BY 4.0:

  • fps animated smg by DJMaesen: native gloved hands, support-arm rig, sleeves and textures. Adapted by AetherRadar / Operation Steel Tide.
  • M4A1 by Firewarden3D (Firewarden): rifle model and textures.
  • Generic Red Dot Scope / Rifle Attachment Lowpoly by valterjherson1: optic model and textures.
  • cktang88 / 3d-fps: GLB conversion and optic preparation. Tideline selects the native runtime support forearm and fixed trigger hand, removes rejected fitted arms and unused geometry, embeds textures, extracts the micro optic, adjusts materials, fits the camera and sight, and adds support-arm positioning, reload motion, reticle, riser and suppressor. No hand vertices are warped.

Software and original work

Three.js and Vite are MIT-licensed. Mission code, environment geometry, procedural fallback characters and weapons, first-person reload choreography, attachments and synthesized audio were authored for Tideline.

All runtime assets are bundled locally. Exact source revisions, checksums and adaptation details are recorded in the repository's THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md and asset manifests.
